# BuyAnAnswer — plain-language overview

**Your link in bio, but every tip buys a real answer.**

BuyAnAnswer is a paid question-and-answer app. Fans pay to ask a creator a question, and the
creator gets paid to answer. The money is held safely onchain and only reaches the creator
when they actually answer — if they don't, the asker gets it all back.

- **Money:** USDC (a US-dollar stablecoin) on **Base**, an Ethereum Layer-2 network.
- **Non-custodial:** payments are held by a smart contract, not by BuyAnAnswer. We never touch your funds or keys.
- **You only pay for answers:** declined or ignored questions are refunded in full.

## How it works

1. **Claim your link.** Grab `buyananswer.com/you` and set your minimum price. Your handle is yours.
2. **Share it anywhere.** Put it in your bio, a cast, or a story. Fans tap through to ask.
3. **Answer & get paid.** Reply to unlock the payment. Your USDC lands in your balance — withdraw to your wallet anytime.

## For creators

- Set your own price — charge what your time is worth.
- Only answer what's worth it; decline the rest (the asker is refunded in full).
- The money is already there — it's held the moment someone asks, before you even open the question.
- Withdraw anytime, straight to your wallet. Your funds, always in your control.

## For askers

- Your USDC is **held, not spent** — enforced by the blockchain, not a promise.
- **Refunded in full** if the creator declines, or if they don't answer within 7 days.
- A real answer from a real person — skip the ignored DM.
- Keep it private, or let the creator publish it as a shareable public card (you're shown only by wallet address).

## The money rules

| Action | Who | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask | Fan | USDC is held onchain for the creator |
| Answer | Creator | Creator is paid the amount minus a **4.2%** fee; the answer is revealed |
| Decline | Creator | Asker is refunded **100%** |
| Cancel (before the 7-day deadline) | Asker | Asker is refunded, minus a **1%** fee |
| Reclaim (after the deadline, unanswered) | Anyone | Asker is refunded **100%** |
| Withdraw | Anyone with a balance | Moves your held balance to your own wallet |

## FAQ

**What is BuyAnAnswer?**
A paid Q&A app: fans pay to ask a creator a question in USDC on Base, the money is held
onchain, and it's released to the creator only when they answer — refunded if they don't.

**Do I need crypto to use it?**
You need an Ethereum wallet with a little USDC on Base. You connect your wallet and sign in with
it — there's no separate account or password. Payments settle in USDC, a dollar-pegged stablecoin.

**What if a creator never answers?**
You don't lose anything. If they decline, you're refunded in full immediately. If they don't
answer within 7 days, you can take your money back. You're only ever charged for a real answer.

**How much does it cost?**
Creators set their own minimum price. BuyAnAnswer charges a 4.2% fee only on answered questions —
the creator keeps the rest. Declining is free; cancelling before the deadline costs 1%.

**Is my money safe?**
Yes. Funds are held by a smart contract on Base, not by BuyAnAnswer — it's non-custodial, so we
can never touch your money. You withdraw your balance to your own wallet whenever you want.

**Can I make my answer public?**
Optionally. After answering, a creator can publish the Q&A as a shareable card; the asker is shown
only by wallet address. Otherwise it stays private between the two parties.

**What chain and currency does it use?**
Base (an Ethereum Layer-2) for settlement, and USDC for payment. Gas fees are low.

**Is it a subscription?**
No. Every question is a one-off paid request — you pay per answer, not per month.
